###An urn contains 4 red balls , 5 green balls and 6 white balls. If one ball is drawn at random, find the probability that it is neither red nor white?

Solution -  Given that , 

Red balls = 4
Green Balls = 5   
White Balls = 6

P = 5^C1 / 15^C1
P = n                         ***So only one bal is drawn then n^C1 = n
P = 5/15
Answer = (1/3)
